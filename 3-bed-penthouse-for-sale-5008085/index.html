DNG is delighted to present this exceptional, three-bedroom corner penthouse apartment to the market. Spanning approximately 115 sq m (1,238 sq ft), this residence boasts a predominantly southerly orientation with views overlooking the grounds of Castleknock College.

The property is presented in excellent condition, featuring a high standard of finish throughout. The well-proportioned accommodation comprises an entrance hall, a spacious living/dining room, and a dedicated kitchen/breakfast room. There are three double bedrooms, including a master with en-suite, and a separate main shower room.

A standout feature of this penthouse is its enviable southerly aspect; four private balconies (29 sq m/312 sq ft combined) offer serene views of the surrounding communal grounds and gardens. For added convenience, the property includes one designated parking space. Additionally, there is designated visitor parking available.

Developed by Kimpton Vale Ltd in 2003 on the former grounds of Castleknock College, Collegewood is a highly sought-after development located just off Carpenterstown Road. It is a short stroll from Castleknock Village via pedestrian access through College Grove and is within walking distance of both the Phoenix Park and Castleknock Train Station at Laurel Lodge.

The location also offers easy access to the Blanchardstown Centre, James Connolly Memorial Hospital, and the National Aquatic Centre. Excellent transport links are provided by the 37 and 38 high-frequency bus routes to Dublin City Centre, while the N3, M3, and M50 road networks are all highly accessible.

Viewing is highly recommended.
